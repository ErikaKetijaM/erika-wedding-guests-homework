How to use this app

  1. Select a demonstration role and submit a sale or expense with a unique reference.
  2. Svetlana approves pending records and can adjust a commission split or expense allocation.
  3. Each change is saved in Supabase, sent to the linked Telegram account when available, and synchronized to Google Sheets.
